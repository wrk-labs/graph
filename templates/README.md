# Graph

A personal knowledge hub, kept as plain files.

- `inbox/` — what has arrived and is not yet placed
- `people/` — the people in your life
- `organizations/` — companies, institutions, groups, and what happens inside them
- `finance/` — money: accounts, records, taxes
- `research/` — what you are investigating now
- `knowledge/` — what you have learned and want to keep
- `archive/` — what is over but worth keeping
- `journal/` — what happened, by date; `journal/agents/` is the agents' own log

Each directory explains itself in its own `README.md`. The conventions — how
entries are shaped, named and linked — are in [[AGENTS.md]], written for the
agents that work here and just as useful to people.
