# Research

What you are investigating right now. Open questions, reading in progress,
experiments, comparisons, drafts — the work of finding out, before you know.
The question, where it stands, what was learned along the way, and the
sources.

Research that belongs to an organization or project may live there instead.
When a question is answered, what was learned goes to `knowledge/`; when an
inquiry is over, it goes to `archive/`. Research is alive; if something here
has not moved in a long time, it is one or the other.
