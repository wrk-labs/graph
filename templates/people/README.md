# People

The people in your life: family, friends, colleagues, clients, contacts —
anyone you keep track of. Who they are, how you know them, how to reach them,
what is going on with them, and the files that are about them.

You are a person too. Your own documents — identification, health, education,
anything that is about you rather than about what you do — belong here as
much as anyone else's.

What a person does inside an organization belongs with the organization,
linked back to the person.
