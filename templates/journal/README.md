# Journal

Where time lives. The rest of the repository is organized by thing — a
person, an organization, a subject; the journal is organized by when. What
happened, what was decided, what you noticed, what is on your mind.

How you keep it is yours to choose — by day, by week, by theme, in one
running file. Link entries to the things they touch, and the journal becomes
the history of everything else: each entity's page shows the days it came up.

`journal/agents/` is kept separately, by the agents that work here.
